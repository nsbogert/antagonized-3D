MARIN’S FIELD GUIDE

Know your little enemies.

01 / STOMP → AMMO

Press Space near a worker: the landing ring and a gentle pull help line up your stomp. Walk over a squashed worker in throw or ant-cannon mode to load it automatically when there is room. Spray and foam leave carcasses on the ground. Pale eggs in the colony load the same way, sharing the one-in-hand or three-in-cannon capacity. Shift throws the next ant or egg; eggs splat on impact and can stagger foamed soldiers. Steer sideways or backward to cancel jump assistance. Three defeats unlock the cannon at the tool bench.

02 / FOAM → RIDE

Foam a soldier, then hit it with a carcass or stomp its back. Press E to mount. Walk into the ivy planter to climb. Shift to bite, or hold C to charge.

03 / BAIT → DISTRACT

Carry one trap at a time. Q drops a lure that lasts 25 seconds. Workers leave their food trails to investigate. Press E at the tool bench or field supplies to refill your single bait slot. Sealing a food cache also restores that slot; bait never stacks. Draw them away, then approach a food cache and press E to seal it.

04 / TOOLS WITH A JOB

Pick up the loaded spray + foam kit at the tool bench—available immediately. Spray: a powerful, wide short-range cone. Foam: hold soldiers still for 14 seconds and ground flyers. Mist: a lingering damage cloud, unlocked after two caches. The tank refills automatically when empty or after a short break in firing. The bench restores health and bait.
